01 · The company

A sourcing and product development house in Dhaka

We act as the local partner for international brands and retailers, managing the full order cycle from initial design and sampling through to production follow-up, quality assurance and final inspection.

Our team brings more than sixteen years of hands-on experience across denim, non-denim woven, circular knit, flat knit and outerwear. Production is placed with a vetted base of compliant manufacturing partners, so every order is matched to the right factory for its product type, quantity and price point.

Studio Apparels is authorised to carry out final inspection on behalf of its buyers — letting clients reduce overhead in Bangladesh without giving up control over product quality.

What makes us different
  • One team from tech pack to shipment, with no handovers between agents
  • Factory selection matched to each product type rather than fixed to one unit
  • Buyer-authorised final inspection carried out from our own office

To be a globally trusted apparel sourcing partner, delivering quality, innovation, sustainability and value to fashion brands worldwide.

Quality assurance

How we protect quality

01 · Pre-production

Fabric and trim inspection on receipt, pre-production meeting with the factory, and size set approval before bulk cutting begins.

02 · In-line

Checks at cutting, sewing and finishing while the order is on the line, so defects are caught and corrected before they multiply.

03 · Final

AQL-based final random inspection on packed cartons, covering measurement, workmanship, labelling and packing accuracy.

04 · Reporting

Photo-supported inspection reports shared with the buyer, with a clear pass, hold or re-inspect recommendation.

Brands can release shipments without sending their own QA team to Bangladesh.
